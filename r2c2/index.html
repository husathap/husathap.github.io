About the Lab

R2/C2 is a lab at Algoma University, founded around 2026, aiming to research how make extended reality (XR) technologies more affordable, more available, and more resilient to real world uses. Currently, there is not much work at this point - except for the Fantôme analysis, which aims to introduce a new and robust trajectory analyisis method for extended reality data. R2/C2 also provides general training in XR for Algoma University students as well. Therefore, Algoma University students are welcome to reach out to Prof. Hu for XR work - even though the scopes may not fit R2/C2's goal. Additionally, the lab also collaborates both internally and externally with multiple researchers and institutions.

Current Projects

  • Extended Reality (XR) Planetarium: Currently, Prof. Ryan Chown and I are supervising a Bachelor's degree student on developing an XR-based planetarium. Since the projects are still early in development, we are not looking for students to update the project.
  • Robust Spatial Analysis Techniques: the Fantôme analysis will require more work to make it more effective. However, it is important to note that due to the lack of funding, the project will not be extended. However, Prof. Hu is willing to co-supervise a student who is interested in expanding this method.

Past Projects

The following projects are no longer being developed and maintained.

  • ARTIV (Including Gander and ViSSTA): ARITV is an umbrella of projects, including Gander and ViSSTA at Dalhouse University. ARTIV is not part of the current R2/C2 work. Please note that software will require a significant rewrite, and the target hardware (Microsoft HoloLens) is no longer being manufactured.
  • AR-based Interactive Digital Narratives (AR-IDNs): While the data analysis works with the AR-IDNs continue. The project itself is no longer being maintained.
