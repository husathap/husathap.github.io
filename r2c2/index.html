About the Lab

R2/C2 is a lab at Algoma University, founded around 2026, aiming to research how make extended reality (XR) technologies more affordable, more available, and more resilient to real world uses. Currently, there is not much work at this point - except for the Fantôme analysis, which aims to introduce a new and robust trajectory analyisis method for extended reality data. Additionally, the lab also collaborates both internally and externally with multiple researchers and institutions.

Members and Collaborators

Founder: Prof. Sathaporn "Hubert" Hu

Professor Hu (aka. สถาพร ฮู, 胡秀楷) is an assistant professor at Algoma University, Sault Ste. Marie. He obtained his PhD from Dalhousie University. For more information, please visit the homepage.

Collaborators

Since September 2026, Prof. Hu has been collaborating with Prof. Ryan Chown on an extended reality (XR) planetarium project. Both are supervising an undergradaute student named Mantej Nagra. In the past, Prof. Hu also collaborated with the following:

  • March 2026 - October 2026: Prof. Hu collaborated with Prof. Somang Nam to publish an undergraduate thesis. The work (draft) was accepted by IEEE ISEMV'26 and it was presented by an undergraduate student supervised by Prof. Nam in Larnaca, Cyprus.
  • May 2026: Prof. Hu invited J Tseng and Mo Ayato of StudioSIAT, Simon Fraser University to provide a talk on virtual streaming.
  • May 2025 - August 2026: Prof. Hu collaborated with Prof. Muhammad Azam and Prof. Nam on an immersive analytics project which aims to visualize machine learning algorithms in immersive 3D. The project predates the founding of R2/C2. Profs. Hu, Azam and Nam supervised Harris Adedeji, an undergradaute student from September 2025 to August 2026. Meet Shekhada was supervised by Profs. Hu and Azam from May 2025 to August 2025.
